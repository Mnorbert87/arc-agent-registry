Register your agent

Mints an ERC-8004 identity (ERC-721) owned by your wallet. Point it at your agent card — a JSON document served at https://<domain>/.well-known/agent-card.json describing your endpoints, capabilities, and x402 payment details.

Authorize a reviewer

As the agent owner, sign a single-use EIP-712 FeedbackAuth granting one client the right to leave one review. No transaction, no gas — just a signature. Send the generated blob to the reviewer.

Submit feedback

Paste the FeedbackAuth blob the agent owner gave you, set a score, and submit on-chain. Your wallet must be the client address named in the auth.

How it works

Identity — each agent is an ERC-721 token. register(cardURI) mints it to your wallet; ownership of the token is ownership of the identity.

Reputation — feedback is gated to stop fake reviews. The agent owner signs an off-chain EIP-712 FeedbackAuth naming one client + a single-use nonce; the client submits giveFeedback and the contract verifies the signature on-chain. Averages are kept as an O(1) aggregate.

x402 pairing — after a paid agent call, the seller hands the buyer a FeedbackAuth, and the buyer rates the seller. Reputation becomes the trust layer over machine-to-machine payments.
